      Missable trophies — 11 to watch

      “Missable” here means the trophy belongs to one specific story moment or one specific move. None of them is lost forever: a story mission can be replayed from the pause menu, and a one-off move can be retried until it lands. Grab these while the moment is in front of you and you will never need the safety net.

        How the tracker works

        Tick a trophy and it is saved in your browser (localStorage) — no account, no network, nothing leaves your machine. Export downloads your progress as a JSON file; import reads one back. The spoiler setting hides the names and descriptions of story and mission trophies until you earn them. Everything on this page runs from its own files with the network off.

        A note on honesty

        GTA VI launched on 2026-11-19 and this guide was built a day later. The trophy list itself is official and frozen; the guides are our own reading of those descriptions. If a trigger turns out different in the game, the guide will say so — a cited guide that admits what it does not know beats a confident one that invents a location.
